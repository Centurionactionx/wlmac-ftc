Two seasons in, we've already grown to two teams.

WLMAC Robotics is the student-run FIRST Tech Challenge program at William Lyon Mackenzie CI. We field two competitive teams, build every robot from scratch, and run STEM outreach across the region. A year in FTC takes students through the full engineering cycle, from design reviews and fabrication to code sprints and competition strategy. By the end, they have real hardware to show for it.

Students planned and ran every part of the work on this page, from the robots and outreach to the budget. The school gives us a home, and sponsors make the season possible.

FIRST Tech Challenge is an international robotics league where student teams design and build 18-inch robots for a new game each year. They compete in alliances, but the season is built in the shop.

27964

CyberLyons

CyberLyons is our founding team. It set the standard for the program: build it yourself, test until something breaks, and arrive ready to compete.

32514

MechLyons

MechLyons is our second team and the clearest sign that the program is growing. The same shop and standards now give twice as many students a chance to build, drive, and compete each season.

Outreach

Across ten events and three partner schools, we've reached 200+ students through the Mackenzie Science and Engineering Olympics, Library Open House, Grade 9 Night, and community workshops across North York. Each event gives more local students a way into STEM.

Competition

FTC gives students engineering they can hold, run, and break. In our second season, both teams took months of build work to 6+ competitions, testing their code, strategy, and driving against the region's best.

What sponsors actually get.
